Accessibility & Social Impact

Many people with hearing or speech difficulties struggle to be understood in everyday situations. SignSpeak AI explores how a camera and lightweight AI can turn isolated gestures that you train into on-screen text and spoken words, and lets communities build vocabularies for their own signs. It is a support tool and an educational prototype. It does not translate full sign-language sentences or grammar, and it rejects unfamiliar poses instead of guessing.

      Camera diagnostics
      

      Camera setup guide: laptop · USB phone · DroidCam

      Laptop: click Start Camera, allow permission. If it fails, close other apps using the webcam and check OS privacy settings.
      
Phone over USB: a plain USB cable does not expose the camera to browsers. Use a feature/app that creates a virtual webcam (e.g. DroidCam USB, or Windows' "Link to Windows"/Camo-type apps).
      
DroidCam: install the DroidCam app on the phone and the DroidCam Client (with driver) on the PC. USB: enable USB debugging on the phone, plug in, open the app, choose the USB tab in the PC client, Start. Wi-Fi: same network, enter the IP/port shown in the phone app, Start. Keep the client running, then press Refresh Cameras and pick "DroidCam Source" (name varies).
      
Not listed? Restart the browser after installing the driver, check no other app holds the camera, and confirm the client says "connected". The app never selects another camera without telling you.
